# PATHPILOT — A Premium EdTech Platform 

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

Students often study without knowing which concepts they truly understand, where their knowledge gaps are, or what they should learn next.

PathPilot addresses this problem by presenting a structured learning experience.

The platform helps students:
- Discover their knowledge gaps.
- Understand their topic-wise mastery.
- Identify prerequisite concepts they need to improve.
- Follow a personalized learning roadmap.
- Track their learning progress.
- Practice through interactive assessments.

The main product philosophy:

**"Don't just study more. Learn what matters next."**
